## 🆓 Part 3: **Azure Free Tier Account**

The **Azure Free Tier** allows you to explore and test Azure services **at no cost**. It’s ideal for beginners, students, and professionals who want to learn cloud computing hands-on.

---

### 🔹 **1. Setting up a Free Azure Account**

Follow these steps to create your free Azure account:

1. **Visit:** [https://azure.microsoft.com/free](https://azure.microsoft.com/free)
2. Click on **"Start Free"** or **"Start for Free"** button.
3. **Sign in** with your Microsoft account (or create one).
4. Enter required **personal information** (name, country, etc.).
5. Provide **credit card details** for identity verification (No charges unless you upgrade).
6. Accept the **terms and conditions**.
7. Complete the signup and access the **Azure Portal** at [https://portal.azure.com](https://portal.azure.com)

> 🔐 **Note:** You won't be charged unless you manually upgrade your subscription.

---

---

### 🔹 **3. Cost Management & Budget Setup**

Azure provides built-in tools to help monitor and control costs:

- **Azure Cost Management + Billing**
  - Track spending
  - Set alerts and budgets
  - Forecast usage

- **Steps to Set a Budget:**
  1. Go to the **Azure Portal**
  2. Search for **"Cost Management + Billing"**
  3. Navigate to **"Budgets"**
  4. Click on **"Add"**, set amount and notification thresholds

- **Enable Spending Alerts** for your free credit:
  - Get email alerts when you hit 50%, 75%, or 100% of your $200 credit

---

### 🔐 **Important Notes**
- Azure Free Account expires after **30 days** or after you use your **$200 credit**, whichever comes first.
- After the free credit is used, you must **upgrade manually** to a pay-as-you-go subscription.
- Always monitor usage to avoid accidental billing if you move beyond free limits.
